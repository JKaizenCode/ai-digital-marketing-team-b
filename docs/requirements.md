# ViriVR House Party Landing Pages — Requirements

**Project:** Digital Marketing Centre of Excellence — ViriVR / AppSys VR  
**Purpose:** Working requirements for the three house-party landing pages and the specialist-agent workflow supporting them.  
**Status:** Sprint 2 working document  
**Primary client:** Ajith Ratnarajah  
**Last updated:** September 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

The team is producing **three house-party landing pages** using the coordinated AI-agent workflow.

The objective is to demonstrate a clear supply chain where specialist inputs are produced, reviewed, handed to the relevant role, incorporated into the page, and then taken through client review.

The pages should be treated as **usable landing-page drafts/prototypes for client review**, not automatically as final production pages.

## 2. Page Set

The current working architecture contains three pages:

| ID | Page | Purpose | URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| HP-001 | Bring a Fully Hosted VR House Party to Your Home | Main national commercial landing page for hosted VR house parties | `https://virivr.com.au/house-party/` |
| HP-002 | VR House Parties in Melbourne: We Bring the Experience to You | Local Melbourne service page | `https://virivr.com.au/house-party/melbourne/` |
| HP-003 | House Party Ideas Compared by Age, Space, Budget and Effort | Informational/commercial comparison page that routes qualified users to the hosted VR service | `https://virivr.com.au/house-party/ideas/` |

### Important wireframe clarification

Team A has also produced three wireframe concepts described as:

- Home
- Venue
- Fully Hosted

These should **not automatically be treated as three additional HP pages**.

For the current working architecture, HP-001/002/003 remain the three target pages above. The Home / Venue / Fully Hosted wireframes can be used as **page structures, sections, or service-option concepts** where relevant.

If Ajith confirms that Home / Venue / Fully Hosted are intended to become three separate URLs instead, the architecture must be updated before treating that as the final page structure.

## 3. HP-001 Requirements

### Page

**Title:** Bring a Fully Hosted VR House Party to Your Home

**URL:** `/house-party/`

**Page type:** Commercial national landing page

### Primary keyword

`VR party at home`

### Secondary keywords

- house party VR
- VR house party
- mobile VR party
- VR birthday party at home
- hosted house party entertainment

### Search intent

Commercial investigation / transactional.

The page should answer the organiser's questions quickly and make the service easy to understand and enquire about.

### Canonical purpose

Explain, qualify and convert users looking for a hosted VR house party delivered to their home or another suitable private space.

### Core questions the page should answer

- What is a ViriVR house party?
- How does the hosted VR party work?
- Is the experience brought to my home?
- What types of homes/private spaces are suitable?
- What ages can participate?
- What is the minimum/maximum group size?
- How much space is required?
- What safety requirements apply?
- What power/connectivity is required?
- How does setup and pack-down work?
- How long does the experience take?
- What experiences/games are available?
- What happens if the weather changes?
- Where does ViriVR operate?
- What is included?
- Are food or venue options available?
- What is the difference between a house party, partner venue and private venue option?
- What information is needed to check availability?

### Required content areas

1. **Hero / value proposition**
   - Clear explanation of the hosted VR house-party offer.
   - Clear answer to the user's main question.
   - Strong primary CTA.
2. **How it works**
   - Before the event
   - Arrival/setup
   - VR experience
   - Rotation/participation model
   - Pack-down
3. **Who it is for**
   - Relevant age groups
   - Group sizes
   - Birthday/celebration use cases where supported by approved evidence
4. **Space and safety**
   - Space requirements
   - Safety considerations
   - Power/connectivity requirements where verified
5. **What is included**
   - Equipment
   - Hosting/setup
   - Experiences
   - Other inclusions only where verified
6. **Service options**
   - Home/private-space option
   - Other relevant service/venue pathways
   - Clear distinction between options rather than mixing them together
7. **Service area**
   - National service positioning where supported
   - Link/route to Melbourne page for local detail
8. **Packages / comparison**
   - Only verified pricing or package information
   - Do not invent prices, availability, inclusions or limits
9. **Proof / trust**
   - Testimonials, photos, case studies or other approved proof where available
   - Evidence-backed claims only
10. **FAQ**
    - Concise answer-first responses
    - Questions should reflect real search/user intent
11. **CTA**
    - Primary: check date/postcode/availability
    - Secondary CTA where appropriate
    - CTA should be measurable

### Internal links

Include relevant internal links such as:

- To HP-002: **VR house parties in Melbourne**
- To HP-003: **compare house party ideas**
- To the existing VR party page where appropriate: **compare every ViriVR party option**

## 6. Shared SEO Requirements

All three pages should:

- Have a clear primary search intent.
- Have one clear primary keyword/topic.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Use logical H1/H2/H3 structure.
- Include relevant secondary topics/questions naturally.
- Maintain clear page ownership and avoid cannibalising another page.
- Use appropriate metadata.
- Include relevant internal links.
- Use canonical URLs correctly.
- Use schema/structured-data requirements where applicable.
- Keep claims evidence-grounded.
- Avoid unsupported pricing, availability, capacity or service-area statements.

The page should prioritise **answer-first content** so the main question is answered early.

## 7. AI Search / AEO Requirements

- Provide clear question-and-answer coverage.

## 13. HP-001 First-Review Workflow

The first page should be treated as the initial validation cycle.

1. Team B specialist inputs are prepared.
2. Arnav develops/consolidates the HP-001 draft.
3. HP-001 is handed to the relevant Team A specialists.
4. Team A provides specialist recommendations/changes.
5. Arnav incorporates relevant feedback.
6. HP-001 becomes the **client-review version**.
7. The page is sent to Ajith.
8. Ajith provides feedback/decisions.
9. Feedback is recorded and used to improve HP-002/HP-003 where relevant.

HP-002 and HP-003 may be developed in parallel as drafts, but they should not be treated as client-validated merely because drafts exist.

## 14. General Page Acceptance Criteria

A page is ready for team review when:

- The page has a clear purpose and audience.
- The correct search intent is addressed.
- The primary topic/keyword is clear.
- Core user questions are answered.
- Required page sections are present.
- Claims are supported or explicitly flagged as needing evidence.
- Relevant authority/trust recommendations have been considered.
- Relevant AI-search/AEO recommendations have been considered.
- CTA and customer journey are clear.
- Measurement considerations are identified.
- Internal links are included where required.
- No unsupported price/availability/service-area claims have been added.
- Page ownership/canonical purpose is clear.
- Open questions or unresolved issues are recorded.

## 15. Client Review Requirements

The first client review should focus on:

- Is the page concept correct?
- Does the page accurately represent the ViriVR offer?
- Are Home / Venue / Fully Hosted being represented correctly?
- Are the service options clearly distinguished?
- Are claims, pricing and inclusions accurate?
- Is the CTA appropriate?
- Is the page structure commercially useful?
- Is anything missing?
- Does Ajith want the current HP-001/HP-002/HP-003 architecture kept?
- Does Ajith intend Home / Venue / Fully Hosted to become separate pages instead?

Client decisions should be recorded before treating disputed architecture as final.

## 16. Important Constraints

Do not:

- Invent pricing.
- Invent availability.
- Invent service areas.
- Invent group-size limits.
- Invent testimonials.
- Invent case studies.
- Invent performance results.
- Claim guaranteed rankings.
- Claim guaranteed AI citations.
- Publish unsupported factual claims.
- Merge distinct page intents without confirming the architecture.
- Treat a draft as client-approved.
- Treat specialist recommendations as automatically approved facts.

Where evidence is missing, mark the item as:

`[NEEDS SOURCE]`

Where a decision is required from the client, mark it as:

`[CLIENT CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]`

Where architecture is uncertain, mark it as:

`[ARCHITECTURE CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]`

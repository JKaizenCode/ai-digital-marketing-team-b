# Sprint 2 Week 3 Requirements

## Project
**RMIT Capstone Programming Project 2026 – Team 38**  
**AI Digital Marketing – Team B**

## Sprint
**Sprint 2 – Week 3**

## Objective

Progress HP-001 from reviewed content into the full landing-page stage while beginning early specialist groundwork for HP-002 and HP-003.

The main focus for Week 3 is to support the HP-001 landing-page workflow using Arnav's reviewed content, Mirna's CRO/QA work, Ajith's review, and Team A's landing-page implementation.

---

## Scope

### Primary Focus
- HP-001 full landing-page workflow
- Final content support and clarification
- CRO/QA preparation
- Client review coordination
- Landing-page handoff and implementation support

### Parallel Preparation
- HP-002 specialist groundwork
- HP-003 specialist groundwork

HP-002 and HP-003 are preparation tasks only during this week. They are not expected to be completed as full landing pages during Sprint 2 Week 3.

---

## HP-001 Workflow

The intended Week 3 workflow is:

```text
Arnav HP-001 reviewed content
        ↓
Mirna CRO / QA preparation
        ↓
Ajith review / feedback
        ↓
Hoang Landing Page Agent + Mirna CRO Agent
        ↓
UX / Landing Page Build
        ↓
Full HP-001 Landing Page
        ↓
QA / Content Validation
        ↓
Ajith / Team Review
```

Hoang and Mirna may work in parallel where possible.

## Client Review

Ajith's review remains a key quality gate.

During Week 3:
1. The reviewed HP-001 content is used as the source content.
2. Mirna prepares the QA/CRO requirements.
3. The QA is provided to Ajith for review where required.
4. Feedback is recorded and applied.
5. The full HP-001 landing page is produced.
6. The completed page is validated before final client review.

## Acceptance Criteria

Sprint 2 Week 3 is considered successful when:

- The latest reviewed HP-001 content is clearly identified.
- Mirna's QA/CRO work has been prepared.
- Ajith's relevant feedback has been captured.
- Hoang has the correct content and requirements for landing-page work.
- Full HP-001 landing-page production has progressed.
- Arnav has supported content clarification and corrections.
- HP-002 and HP-003 specialist groundwork has started.
- Minh, Anthony and Chungheng have each produced a combined HP-002/HP-003 specialist output.
- Week 3 handoffs and blockers are documented.
- Outputs are stored or linked in the team repository.
